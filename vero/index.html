We Are on a Mission to Heal Our Planet

Vero Green Initiative is a community-driven environmental organization dedicated to restoring natural habitats, promoting renewable energy, and fostering sustainable development across underserved regions.

Since our founding, we have planted over 50,000 trees, installed solar systems in 20+ communities, and educated thousands on environmental stewardship.
