Our Projects

From reforestation to renewable energy, see the impact of our work across the globe.

Flagship Initiatives

Green Canopy Revival

Amazon Basin, Brazil

Reforesting degraded land with native tree species to restore biodiversity and sequester carbon. 15,000+ trees planted.

Active

Solar for All

Rural Kenya

Deploying off-grid solar systems to power schools, health clinics, and community centers. 45 institutions powered.

Ongoing

Clean Water Access

Coastal Bangladesh

Building rainwater harvesting systems and biosand filters for communities facing water scarcity. 30 systems installed.

Active

Zero Waste Schools

Urban Ghana

Implementing composting and recycling programs in 25 schools, diverting 200+ tons of waste from landfills annually.

Active

Mangrove Restoration

Senegal Delta

Replanting mangrove forests to protect coastlines and support fisheries. 50 hectares restored.

Completed

Youth Climate Action

10 Countries

Training young environmental leaders through workshops and tree-planting campaigns. 3,500+ youth trained.

Ongoing
