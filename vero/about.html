About Us

Our journey, values, and the people behind the mission.

How We Started

2018

Founded

Environmental activists came together to address deforestation and water scarcity in rural Ghana.

2019

10,000 Trees

Restored 50 acres of degraded forest in the Eastern Region of Ghana.

2021

Regional Expansion

Launched programs in Kenya, Ghana, and Senegal across solar energy and clean water.

2026

50,000 Trees & Beyond

Celebrating our 50,000th tree planted with new goals for the next decade.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A world where people and nature thrive in harmony, with healthy ecosystems and sustainable communities for every generation.

Our Mission

To empower communities through reforestation, renewable energy, clean water, and education, creating lasting ecological and social impact.

Our Values

Community First

Local communities own every solution we build.

Sustainability

Long-term impact using renewable resources.

Transparency

Open reporting and honest partnerships.

Empowerment

Equipping people to become environmental stewards.
