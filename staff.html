Our Staff & Faculty

Meet the dedicated team driving academic excellence, groundbreaking research, and student success at TTU-CSD.

Head of Department

Dr. Michael Omari

Head of Department, Computer Science

Dr. Omari leads the department with over 20 years of experience in computer science education and research. His work in distributed systems and cloud computing has been published in over 60 peer-reviewed journals. He is passionate about bridging the gap between academia and industry, ensuring students graduate with both theoretical depth and practical skills.
